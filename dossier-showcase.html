Dossier  |  Luxury resale  ·  Marketplace, web app

A marketplace built on trust, screen by screen.

Resale for bags, shoes, watches and accessories where every piece carries its own authentication record, and money sits in escrow until an independent checkpoint passes it. Below are the 16 core screens across the guest, buyer and seller journeys, each with a short recording of the interaction it was designed around.
