Fluent Conveyors  |  B2B SaaS  ·  Customer portal

The customer portal, screen by screen.

A self-serve portal where facility operators manage conveyor assets, approve quotes, pay invoices, track shipments and stay ahead of maintenance. Below are the 13 core screens, each with a short recording of the interaction it was designed around.

See the case study → Browse the screens ↓

Screen 01 / 13

Dashboard

A single view of fleet health, open work, spend and shipments, so operators see what needs attention first.

Screen 02 / 13

My Assets

Every conveyor and component on site, with status, location and service history, filterable at a glance.

Interaction

Find & view an asset

  1. 1Filter the fleet down to late assets.
  2. 2Open an asset to see its parts, details and spend.

Screen 03 / 13

Asset Details

Deep view of one asset: specifications, maintenance history, related documents and its replacement parts.

Interaction

Add to cart & request a quote

  1. 1Add a replacement part straight from the asset.
  2. 2Submit the cart as a quote request in one step.

Screen 04 / 13

Open Quote Details

Review line items, compare options and approve a quote with a captured signature.

Interaction

Approve a quote

  1. 1Review line items and totals.
  2. 2Add a PO number and sign to accept.

Screen 05 / 13

Invoice Details

Invoice breakdown with tax exemption handling and a guided pay flow from review to paid in full.

Interaction

Pay an invoice

  1. 1Enter card details and apply an exemption.
  2. 2Sign, pay and see the invoice marked paid in full.

Interaction

Upload a tax exemption

  1. 1Choose a reason, number and validity dates.
  2. 2Attach the certificate and submit for review.

Screen 06 / 13

Inventory

Spare parts stock levels across locations, with reorder signals and quick add to cart.

Interaction

Search & update stock

  1. 1Search a part, then open its locations.
  2. 2Adjust stock at a site and confirm the update.

Screen 07 / 13

Shipment Details

Live tracking of an order in transit, from dispatch to delivery, with contents and documents.

Interaction

Track a shipment

  1. 1Progress fills from dispatch to delivery.
  2. 2Tracking history lists every checkpoint.

Screen 08 / 13

Maintenance

Scheduled and reactive work orders in one place, with priority, assignee and due dates.

Interaction

Complete a task

  1. 1Filter to late work orders and open one.
  2. 2Mark the task complete and the order closes.

Screen 09 / 13

Calendar

Service visits, deliveries and shutdowns planned on one calendar with month, week and day views.

Interaction

Open a day

  1. 1Select a day to see its events.
  2. 2Move to next month without losing context.

Screen 10 / 13

Help Desk

Raise and follow support tickets, with status, priority and response times clearly tracked.

Interaction

Raise a ticket

  1. 1Open New Ticket and fill in subject, asset and part.
  2. 2Set urgency, describe the issue and submit.

Screen 11 / 13

Ticket Details

The full conversation on a ticket, with attachments, status changes and the assigned engineer.

Screen 12 / 13

Fluent AI

An assistant that answers questions about assets, orders and maintenance in plain language.

Interaction

Ask Fluent AI

  1. 1Type a question in plain language.
  2. 2The answer card streams in with sources.

Screen 13 / 13

E-Learning

Training courses for operators and technicians, with progress tracking and certificates.
