SmartLearning UK  |  EdTech  ·  Learning platform, web app

Four portals, screen by screen.

One learning platform for learners, trainers, admins and employers: buy a course, learn live, get assessed, get paid. Below are the 27 core screens across the four portals, each with a short recording of the interaction it was designed around.
